How Raqeb works

Five stages that already share their data.

A request becomes tasks, tasks consume capacity, capacity is recorded as time, and time is what margin is made of. Break that chain and you get a monthly surprise. Raqeb keeps it whole.

01

A request arrives

Scope, owner and the first real dates, before anyone promises a week.

02

Work moves

Board, list and timeline share one set of stages, so status is never retyped.

03

Capacity answers

Demand meets who is actually free, with leave and part time already counted.

04

Time lands on the task

A timer that runs where the work happens, not a Friday memory exercise.

05

Margin becomes visible

Utilization, billability and profit read from the same hours you already logged.

Project delivery

Nothing quietly slips.

Every task carries its owner, stage, estimate and history, so the answer to "where is this" takes one glance instead of three messages.

The board tells you what is late

Overdue, blocked and ownerless work is flagged on the card, not buried in a filter.

The whole record opens in place

Brief, subtasks, files, comments and logged time in a drawer, never a page you have to come back from.

Board, list or timeline, one truth

Switch the view without rebuilding it. The stages, fields and permissions travel with you.
