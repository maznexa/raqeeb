Before you ask

The questions that decide it.

Is this employee monitoring?

No, and it is built so it cannot become that. Raqeb measures work: tasks, estimates, hours against a project. It does not log keystrokes, capture screens, track idle time or produce a productivity score for a person. Managers see load so they can move work, not so they can rank people.

We already use a project tool. Why change?

Most teams do not have a project tool problem, they have a seam problem. The board says green, the spreadsheet says the job lost money, and the two never meet. If your delivery, capacity and margin already live in one place and reconcile, you do not need Raqeb. If they do not, that seam is what this is for.

How long does it take to get real value?

A team can run their first week of work in an afternoon. Capacity planning is useful once contracted hours and leave are in, usually a few days. Margin reporting needs a month of logged time before the numbers are worth acting on, because it reads from real hours rather than an assumption.

Who can see cost and profitability?

Only the roles you grant. It is enforced on the server, so a user without the role cannot retrieve the data even if they know the address. Most teams give it to owners, finance and account leads, and nobody else notices it exists.

What about Arabic?

Raqeb is designed bilingual, with a full right to left interface rather than a mirrored English one. The Arabic release ships this quarter. If you plan to roll out in Arabic first, tell us in the walkthrough and we will show you the current state honestly.

What happens if we stop paying?

Your workspace becomes read only and export keeps working. We do not lock data behind a lapsed invoice, and we do not pause automations to force an upgrade.

The Raqeb principle

Watch the work, never the people.

Plenty of tools in this category sell the opposite. Here is what we will not build, so you can hold us to it.

  • Keystroke and activity logging
  • Screenshot capture
  • Idle and away timers
  • Productivity scores for individuals
  • Private manager only surveillance reports

Raqeb measures work, not people. That is a product decision, not a setting an administrator can quietly switch on.
