Access and governance

Cost and margin are visible to the people you choose. Nobody else.

Financial visibility is the reason a tool like this gets rejected. Raqeb treats it as a permission boundary enforced on the server, not a hidden column in the interface.

Role based access, enforced server side

Rates, cost and profitability are gated by role at the API, so hiding a screen is not the control. A user without the role cannot request the data at all.

Every workspace is isolated at the database

Tenant separation is enforced by row level security on every query, not by a filter the application remembers to apply.

Traceable history

Task changes, time entries and approvals keep who did what and when, so an operating review or an audit does not depend on memory.

Clients and guests never take a paid seat

Reviewers see only what you share with them. It is a database constraint, not a pricing promise we could quietly change later.

Your data leaves whenever you want

Export works on every plan, including a lapsed one. If billing stops, the workspace becomes read only rather than locked.

Built in Riyadh, planned for Saudi requirements

Raqeb can be planned to support applicable Saudi data protection, governance and residency requirements. Exact controls and deployment scope are confirmed during enterprise planning.
