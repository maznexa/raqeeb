RAQEB (رقيب) - marketing site, English and Arabic
==================================================

TO LOOK AT IT
-------------
Double click index.html for the English page.
Double click ar.html for the Arabic page (right to left).
The "AR / EN" switch in the header moves between the two, so keep both files
in this same folder.

Nothing to install, nothing to build, no server. Each page is one file with
its CSS and JavaScript inside it. Everything works offline except the three
fonts (Inter, Inter Tight, IBM Plex Sans Arabic), which load from Google Fonts
when you are online.

TO PUT IT ONLINE
----------------
Upload this folder to any static host (Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, S3, nginx).
index.html at the root, ar.html beside it. That is the whole deployment.

WHAT IS IN HERE
---------------
index.html      the English homepage
ar.html         the Arabic homepage, full RTL
README.md       what the folder is and the rules the pages follow
HANDOFF.md      every decision, open question and instruction for the next round
brand/          the RAQEB wordmark and the Q app icon as SVG, light and dark
screenshots/    reference renders, desktop and mobile, both languages
build/          the source parts the English page was assembled from
tools/          Playwright scripts that check contrast, overflow and RTL parity
audits/         the critic audit that drove the rebuild
